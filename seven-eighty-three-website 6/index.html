Thesis

783 studies how people and organizations decide what to build, and whether that process can be made legible. It treats human interaction as a system with observable inputs and outputs.

Research question

Can the tacit signals practitioners use to make decisions be observed, documented, and translated into repeatable tools?

Hypothesis: actors, founders, and other practitioners already act on timing, audience response, and interpersonal resonance. The data is real but tacit. 783 makes it explicit without extracting from it.

Method

  1. 1. Input

    A participant brings a question. 783 engages by invitation, not intervention.

  2. 2. Processing

    Sustained observation in a low-noise research environment, using the property walk method.

  3. 3. Output

    Patterns of timing, resonance, and decision-making, translated into frameworks and software prototypes.

This is the observation layer of the Universe API. Its index layer, under Infrastructure Design, structures how AI and search engines read the results.

What 12 months delivers

  • Field notes and case studies from 1:1 Talent Architect sessions.
  • A lightweight 783 prototype that helps founders state their central question and track resonance over time.
  • Documentation of the non-extractive model, as a contribution to organizational studies.
