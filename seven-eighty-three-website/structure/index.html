How Lab 783 is structured

Seven Eighty Three, known as Lab 783, is a venture foundry that births infrastructure and whole companies. Wild Lake Studio is its research laboratory, created within it. Communal Foundation is its first company.

Lab 783

THE VENTURE FOUNDRY. OPEN TO INVESTMENT AND ACQUISITION.

Lab 783 came first, and it makes the method scalable. It builds infrastructure and whole companies, and builds them to sell. Its work includes AI-assisted tools for human-systems mapping, founder calibration, and organizational resonance calibration, each drawn from the research done at Wild-Lake. Its first company, Communal Foundation, was built between September and November 2026.

WILD-LAKE

THE RESEARCH LABORATORY. NOT FOR SALE.

Wild-Lake keeps the method honest. Wild Lake Studio is a brutalist talent architecture and structural design studio. It scouts raw creative capability and builds systems around it, across songs, scripts, and major bodies of work. It formalizes a fifteen-year independent practice of guiding developing artists.

Created within Lab 783 as its research laboratory, Wild-Lake is the home of the Talent Architecture practice: advisory on a retainer or fixed project fee, covering artist development, A&R, and creative infrastructure. It takes no ownership or percentage of an artist's work.

Wild-Lake also partners with record labels, galleries, and booking agencies. Every engagement begins with the artist's willing participation, whether they approach directly or through a representative.

COMMUNAL FOUNDATION

THE FIRST COMPANY. AVAILABLE FOR ACQUISITION.

A systems laboratory that pairs hyper-local demographic constraints with chef-driven culinary artistry. Lab 783 built it between September and November 2026 and packaged it as a turnkey venture.
