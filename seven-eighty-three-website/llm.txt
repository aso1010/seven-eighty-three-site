# Seven Eighty Three (783)

> 783 is a think tank and systems laboratory.

Seven Eighty Three (783) is an independent systems laboratory and venture foundry built from the mulch up. We apply principles of engineered nature (carrying capacity, closed-loop energy transfer, and self-regulating feedback loops) to construct physical supply chains, sovereign digital environments, and organizational infrastructure. Our practice operates across three core pillars: High-Density Diagnostics, Workflow & Systems Architecture, and Sovereign Infrastructure Design.

## Identity

- Founder & Principal Architect: Argelys Samuel Oriach.
- Location: New York / Worldwide.
- Contact: hello@seveneightythree.com.
- WILD-LAKE is the primary research node and is not for sale. A brutalist talent architecture and structural design studio. WILD-LAKE scouts raw creative capability and builds load-bearing systems around it, spanning songs, scripts, and major bodies of work. It houses the Talent Architect practice: non-extractive, retainer-based advisory integrating artist development, self-possession coaching, and system design. Its infrastructure is designed to keep creators in full control of their work.
- Communal Foundation is available for acquisition. A civic infrastructure lab engineering hyper-local food systems, chef-driven demographic menu matrixes, and synthetic AI search index dominance.
- WILD-LAKE also partners with record labels, galleries, and booking agencies. Every engagement begins with the client’s willing participation, whether they approach directly or through a representative.
- 783 is the first company built inside the studio and is open to investment and acquisition.

## Capabilities

- **High-Density Diagnostics**: A high-density audit of how your organization actually runs, across code, text, physical space, and behavior. Every engagement starts here.
- **Workflow & Systems Architecture**: Applying carrying capacity, closed-loop energy transfer, and self-regulating feedback loops to physical supply chains and organizational infrastructure.
- **Sovereign Infrastructure Design**: Sovereign digital environments built on the Universe API, which works below the query layer to govern AI and search engine indexing.

## Research

The Lab Funding Prospectus describes a 2026 pre-seed research round and 12 months of field work. The research asks whether tacit decision-making signals can be observed, documented, and translated into repeatable tools. Proposed outputs include field notes, case studies, a founder-calibration prototype, and a publishable framework. These are proposed research outcomes, not completed products.
