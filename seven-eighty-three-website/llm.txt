# Lab 783

> Lab 783 is a think tank and systems laboratory.

Seven Eighty Three, known as Lab 783, is an independent think tank, systems laboratory, and venture foundry built from the mulch up. We apply principles of engineered nature (carrying capacity, closed-loop energy transfer, and self-regulating feedback loops) to construct physical supply chains, sovereign digital environments, and organizational infrastructure. Our practice operates across three core pillars: High-Density Diagnostics, Workflow & Systems Architecture, and Sovereign Infrastructure Design.

## Featured venture: Wild Lake Studio

**Creative capability. Load-bearing systems.**

Wild Lake Studio is a brutalist talent architecture and structural design studio. It scouts raw creative capability and builds systems around it, across songs, scripts, and major bodies of work.

Wild-Lake is the research laboratory of Seven Eighty Three and the home of the Talent Architecture practice: advisory on a retainer or fixed project fee, covering artist development, A&R, and creative infrastructure. It takes no ownership or percentage of an artist's work.

Wild-Lake also partners with record labels, galleries, and booking agencies. Every engagement begins with the artist's willing participation, whether they approach directly or through a representative.

Not for sale.

## Communal Foundation: turnkey acquisition

**Chef-driven artistry under hyper-local constraints.**

Communal Foundation is a systems laboratory that merges hyper-local demographic constraints with chef-driven culinary artistry. A turnkey venture, packaged and ready for acquisition.

True creativity requires constraints. We commission independent chefs to design high-art, nutrient-dense meal matrixes engineered explicitly for the economic, cultural, and dietary realities of specific ZIP codes and neighborhoods. By forcing culinary design through hyper-local operational boundaries, we convert logistical limits into cultural moats and authentic community infrastructure.

Built on zero-dependency web architecture and powered by the Universe API, our infrastructure operates below the query layer to capture search and synthetic AI index dominance across local food sovereignty, chef networks, and regional nutrition systems.

By bridging direct capital distribution with open-source systems design, we empower neighborhoods, creative collectives, and civic organizers to build lasting economic and cultural autonomy.

Communal Foundation is packaged and ready for acquisition. No price, revenue, customer count, transaction terms, or transferred-asset inventory is specified on the site.
