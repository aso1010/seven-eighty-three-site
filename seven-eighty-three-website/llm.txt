Wild-Lake keeps the method honest. Wild Lake Studio is a brutalist talent architecture and structural design studio. ## Communal Foundation: acquisition and development

Communal Foundation is a food-systems venture built around the Food API specification. Available for acquisition and development. Specification. Not deployed.

**Chef-driven artistry under hyper-local constraints.**

The Food API specification is the core acquisition proposition. It proposes connections between chef networks, neighborhood constraints, hub capacity, pricing, and delivery coverage. A buyer could develop the specification into a product or evaluate integration into an existing food business. The architecture and operating model are proposed, not a deployed meal platform.

Lab 783 developed the venture between September and November 2026. No acquisition price, revenue, customer count, transaction terms, or transferred-asset inventory is specified on the site.

### Proposed integration architecture

The specification proposes connections across three areas:

- Chef network: Chef, Meal and Node records map to chef onboarding, menu briefs and payouts.
- Supply chain: Hub capacity sets each node’s menu. Standard prices follow local income.
- Logistics engine: Every address resolves to one node and one hub. Unserved nodes become a waitlist.

**Status: Specification. Not deployed.** These are proposed connections, not a deployed integration.
