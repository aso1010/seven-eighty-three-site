# Lab 783

> Lab 783 is a think tank and systems laboratory.

Seven Eighty Three, known as Lab 783, is an independent think tank, systems laboratory, and venture foundry built from the mulch up. We apply principles of engineered nature (carrying capacity, closed-loop energy transfer, and self-regulating feedback loops) to construct physical supply chains, sovereign digital environments, and organizational infrastructure. Our practice operates across three core pillars: High-Density Diagnostics, Workflow & Systems Architecture, and Sovereign Infrastructure Design.

## Featured venture: Wild-Lake

Wild-Lake keeps the method honest. Wild Lake Studio is a brutalist talent architecture and structural design studio. It scouts raw creative capability and builds systems around it, across songs, scripts, and major bodies of work. It formalizes a fifteen-year independent practice of guiding developing artists.

Created within Lab 783 as its research laboratory, Wild-Lake is the home of the Talent Architecture practice: advisory on a retainer or fixed project fee, covering artist development, A&R, and creative infrastructure. It takes no ownership or percentage of an artist's work.

Wild-Lake also partners with record labels, galleries, and booking agencies. Every engagement begins with the artist's willing participation, whether they approach directly or through a representative.

Not for sale. [Visit Wild Lake Studio](https://wild-lake.org/). [Wild-Lake and Lab 783](https://seveneightythree.com/structure/).

## Food API: specification for acquisition

**Food systems architecture connecting chef networks, neighborhood needs, and meal-platform operations.**

Food API is a food-systems venture developed by Lab 783, available for acquisition and development.

Its core specification connects chef networks, neighborhood needs, and meal-platform operations. It maps how chef and meal records, local economic and dietary constraints, hub capacity, pricing, and delivery coverage could work together.

Chef-driven artistry under hyper-local constraints is the guiding principle: design food systems around the economic, cultural, and dietary realities of specific ZIP codes and neighborhoods.

Food API provides a proposed architecture for a buyer to develop into a product or evaluate for integration into an existing food business.

Current stage: specification. Not deployed.

The acquisition offer centers on the Food API specification. Supporting assets include the brand and domain, site and infrastructure, and operating model and chef-commissioning framework. This is not a deployed API or live meal-platform integration.

## Research prospectus

The 2026 pre-seed research round proposes 12 months of field work, documentation, and prototype development. Proposed outputs include field notes, a founder-calibration prototype, a publishable framework, and a sellable v1 of Lab 783. These are proposed outcomes, not completed research deliverables. The model is non-extractive and begins with a participant’s question.
