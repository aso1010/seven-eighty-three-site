# Seven Eighty Three (Lab 783)

> It's infrastructure for a life.

We turn your worldview and domain intelligence into a live, owned company.

Seven Eighty Three (Lab 783) is a venture foundry and systems design laboratory. We found and compile sovereign companies for principals whose life story and domain intelligence require custom technical infrastructure rather than consulting decks.

We extract a founder's core worldview and build the live, owned corporate entity from first principles: positioning, brand architecture, zero-dependency web monoliths, LLM discovery pipelines, working software or API engines, and direct commercial rails. Clients retain 100% of their equity by default. An optional Tier 02 advisory warrant requires separate agreement.
