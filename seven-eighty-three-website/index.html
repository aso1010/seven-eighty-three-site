03 // Engagement protocolTwo ways to build.

Every engagement begins with a 60-minute narrative extraction session to identify your advantage and map the technical infrastructure required to monetize it.

Narrative-to-Infrastructure

Tier 01: Narrative & Monolith Architecture

$15,000 to $25,000 flat fee // 0% equity

Best for operators, athletes, and specialists turning domain authority into a direct-to-market storefront. Includes thesis extraction, a custom web monolith, payment or booking rails, and search/LLM indexing. Delivered in 14 to 21 days.

Tier 02: Full Sovereign Venture Compilation

$45,000 to $75,000 flat fee // 0% equity by default

Best for principals launching a software platform or data engine. Includes everything in Tier 01 plus custom interactive tools, proprietary data capture schemas, AI workflow integration, and multi-user onboarding. Delivered in 30 to 45 days.

Optional 2% to 5% advisory warrant, subject to agreement.
